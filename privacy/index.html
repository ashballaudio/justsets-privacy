JustSets Privacy Policy

Publisher: BolaDev, an individual developer.

Privacy and support contact: ashballaudio@gmail.com.

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Your workout data

JustSets stores your exercises, routines, schedules, cycle progress, workout history, set results, body-weight entries and preferences in the app’s local storage. History can also contain comments, ratings, photos already present in saved or imported records, stopwatch laps and GPS details. This information supports workout tracking, history, graphs and planning.

The current app has no account sign-in or developer-operated service for uploading these records. It does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking or a third-party analytics SDK. This does not mean that Apple services or a destination you choose cannot process data as described below.

Location and maps

GPS is optional. With location permission, GPS-enabled workouts use precise location, timestamps, altitude and accuracy information to record routes and calculate distance, pace and elevation. Recording can continue during an eligible workout while the screen is locked or the app is in the background. You can choose GPS Off or stop a recording, and manage location access in iPhone Settings.

Route maps use Apple’s MapKit service, which may contact Apple to obtain map content. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy practices. Viewing a saved route does not start a new GPS recording.

Widget, Lock Screen and Apple Watch

JustSets shares a limited projection of planned workouts and cycle information with its widget through local App Group storage. Workout names, exercise names, set progress and timer information can appear in widgets, Live Activities or alarms, including on the Lock Screen. Consider what is visible to people who can see your device.

The paired Watch companion receives available routine names and active workout state, including reps, weight and timing, through Apple’s WatchConnectivity service. Watch controls send actions back to the iPhone. The iPhone remains responsible for workout execution and saved history; the Watch companion is not an independent workout recorder.

Apple Health and permissions

The iPhone app checks Apple Health availability and authorization status. Its existing HealthKit support is read-only for step count and body mass, but current screens do not provide an action to enable those reads. JustSets does not currently import workouts from, or write workouts to, Apple Health. Workout screens use local or imported workout records; the body-weight screen uses entries you add in JustSets.

JustSets requests location access for optional GPS and alarm authorization for workout countdowns. Local notification and Live Activity behavior also depends on system settings. Timer sounds use audio playback, not microphone recording. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or full photo-library access. You can manage available permissions and Lock Screen visibility in iPhone Settings.

Exporting, importing and sharing

You choose when to share a history export and which destination receives it. JSON includes saved history fields, including GPS coordinates and any saved photos; CSV contains set results and comments with less detail. Check an export before sharing it, especially for precise locations or sensitive comments.

If you enable automatic export and select a folder, JustSets writes updated history snapshots to that folder after successful saves or export-setting changes. A folder can be local or provided by iCloud Drive or another file service; that provider may upload and retain the file under its own terms. Turning automatic export off stops future updates but does not delete existing exported copies.

Import reads the file you select and merges supported history records into local storage. History exports are not complete backups of routines, schedules, settings or cycle state.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local records remain until you change or delete them through the available app controls, or remove the app’s local data. Interrupted or failed-save recovery backups may remain until recovered or explicitly discarded. Some older migration backup data may also remain to protect against data loss. JustSets does not promise automatic repair or complete recovery after termination.

Deleting a visible record does not remove copies you exported, shared or backed up elsewhere, and may not remove retained recovery or legacy copies. Manage exports in Files or with the destination provider and manage device backups through Apple’s settings. The app’s data can be included in device backups according to your system configuration. Uninstalling the app can lose local data and is not a substitute for a verified backup. There is no developer-held account archive that can restore your workouts.

Email support and privacy requests

You can contact BolaDev at ashballaudio@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. When you email us, we receive your sender address, any sender display name you supply, your message and any attachments you choose to include. Correspondence is used to respond to requests, investigate reported problems and meet applicable obligations. It is retained as reasonably necessary for support and applicable obligations; no fixed deletion period is promised.

Email is processed by the email services used to send and receive it. Send only information needed for your request. Please avoid sending a full history export, GPS route, photo or diagnostic log unless it is needed for the issue, and review attachments before sending them. You can ask about access to or deletion of support correspondence by email; any response must take applicable obligations into account. Contacting support does not give BolaDev automatic access to the app’s local records, and a support request cannot erase copies retained by Apple, a file provider or another recipient.

Apple analytics and diagnostics

The app uses system error logging; development builds can retain a bounded local workout-activity diagnostic trace. No third-party analytics SDK or custom diagnostic-upload service is included.

Apple can process usage and diagnostic information under its own services and settings and can make reports available to developers. App Store diagnostic sharing generally depends on user choices; TestFlight testers automatically share crash reports with the developer. These Apple-provided reports are separate from the app’s locally stored workout history.

BolaDev does not currently review Apple analytics, crash reports or TestFlight feedback.

Privacy policy website

The web version of this policy uses GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy practices. The page includes no added analytics, forms, third-party scripts or external fonts. Reading the bundled policy in the app does not visit GitHub Pages.

Policy changes

BolaDev will update this policy when data-handling practices change. The effective date identifies the current version. You can contact ashballaudio@gmail.com with questions about this policy.
